01

Practise by the Water

Roll out mats on our paved deck beside the 16m beach-ledge pool, a level, open space made for sunrise flows and golden-hour classes, with nothing but garden, sky and the sound of the water around you.

Our team clears the pool furniture before class to open up the deck. When the tropical rain arrives, move under cover to the common area, which can also be cleared, and carry on without missing a session.

02

Rain-Ready Common Area

Step through the timber doors into a covered common area that opens onto the garden and pool. Our team clears the furniture for your sessions, so when an afternoon shower rolls in, practice simply moves under cover.

03

A Private Compound

Twelve studios arranged around a single garden and pool create a natural sense of community, while every participant keeps the absolute privacy of their own room. Tucked down a short lane off the main Uluwatu road, it is quiet inside and walkable outside.

04

Rest That Restores

Each 25m² studio has a queen-sized bed in premium linen, blackout curtains, quiet split air-conditioning and its own balcony. Deep sleep is part of the programme, even when you didn't plan it.

November to March

The off-season retreat

Our quietest months are the best time to bring a group to Nema. From November to March, the whole property is easiest to reserve, rates are at their lowest of the year, and Uluwatu itself is calmer.

It is also Bali's wet season, which is gentler here than most people expect. Rain usually arrives in short afternoon bursts rather than all day. The pool stays warm for swimming, our team clears the deck furniture so you can practise in the open between showers, and the covered common area keeps every session on schedule when the rain sets in.

What we provide, what you bring

Clear from the start, so you can plan and price your retreat with confidence.

Nema provides

  • Exclusive use of all 12 studios
  • Queen bed, air-conditioning and balcony in every studio
  • Paved poolside deck for practice, covered common area for rain
  • Pool and common-area furniture cleared by our team for your sessions
  • Custom co-branded yoga mats through our mat partner, on request
  • Housekeeping, staff and 24-hour security
  • 50mbps fiber-optic Wi-Fi
  • Free private parking
  • Villa catering and extra seating on request, through our partners
  • Discounted group dinners: 10–20% off at partner restaurants
  • Transport partners for airport transfers and excursions
  • Fresh coconuts ordered in advance for your group
  • Help designing a local itinerary that fits your budget
  • Scooter rental, and drinks available to buy on site
  • Partnerships with many local services, and our team is happy to help with suggestions
  • Local tips and partner discounts around Uluwatu

You bring

  • Your programme, teachers and facilitators
  • Props and equipment (enquire about our custom mat partner)
  • Your meal plan: villa catering can be arranged on request, or walk to nearby cafes
  • Your activity choices (we can suggest and book through our partners)

Nema does not run retreat activities. Catering at the villas can be arranged on request, partner restaurants offer Nema guests discounted group dinners, and partners can supply extra seating for gatherings.
