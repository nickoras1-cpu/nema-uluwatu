The Best Sunset Spots in Uluwatu

From the Kecak fire dance to clifftop cocktails: where to be when the sun goes down.

By the Nema. Uluwatu team · 23 October 2026 · 5 min read

Uluwatu's coastline faces west, which means one thing: the sun sets over the Indian Ocean every single evening. Where you watch it from is one of the best decisions you will make all day. These are our favourites.

Seven places to watch the sunset

  1. Uluwatu Temple and the Kecak fire dance

    A clifftop temple about 70 metres above the ocean, and a sunset performance of the Kecak fire dance in its amphitheatre. Arrive in the late afternoon to walk the grounds first. Keep sunglasses and phones close: the resident monkeys are famous for snatching them. About 5 to 7 minutes from Nema.

  2. Single Fin

    The original Uluwatu sunset bar, perched above the surf break with live music and a buzzing, easy crowd. Easy to enjoy solo or as a couple. About 4 minutes by ride.

  3. OneEighty

    A glass-bottom infinity pool hanging off the cliffs, and the most dramatic sunset photos in Uluwatu. More champagne than shots.

  4. Ulu Cliffhouse

    A clifftop pool club with low-key DJs, made for a long afternoon that slides into sunset.

  5. El Kabron

    A Spanish-inspired clifftop restaurant and a classic spot for a sunset dinner.

  6. Sundays Beach Club

    For sunset with your toes in the sand, on a white beach at the bottom of the cliffs. Expect a minimum spend.

  7. A quiet beach

    Skip the crowds and walk down to a west-facing beach with a cold coconut. Thomas Beach is a short walk from Nema.

Sunset tips

  • Timing: sunset is roughly between 6pm and 6:30pm all year. Arrive 45 to 60 minutes early.
  • Season: the dry season, roughly April to October, tends to bring the clearest skies.
  • Getting there: book a Grab or Gojek before you go and your ride home before it gets dark. Our front desk can arrange a driver.
  • Minimum spends: many beach and day clubs have one in peak season, so check before you go.

Staying at Nema puts you minutes from most of these, with a short walk to the beach. Show your Nema keyring at partner venues for guest discounts.

Quick Answers

Good to Know

Popular choices include the Kecak fire dance at Uluwatu Temple, Single Fin above the Uluwatu surf break, the glass-bottom pool at OneEighty, Ulu Cliffhouse and El Kabron on the cliffs, and Sundays Beach Club on the sand. For something quieter, head to a west-facing beach.

Because Bali is close to the equator, sunset is roughly between 6pm and 6:30pm all year. Arrive 45 to 60 minutes early for the best seats, especially at popular clifftop venues and Uluwatu Temple.

The Kecak fire dance is performed every evening at the temple's clifftop amphitheatre, timed around sunset (usually around 6pm). It is very popular, so arrive early and expect crowds.

Single Fin is about four minutes by ride and Uluwatu Temple five to seven minutes. Most clifftop venues are a short drive along the coast.
