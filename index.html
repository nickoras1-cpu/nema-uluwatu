A Day at Nema

Slow mornings, golden evenings

  1. 7:00

    Sand before breakfast

    A short walk down the local pathway to Thomas Beach for an early swim in clear turquoise water.

  2. 9:00

    Coffee, then focus

    Brunch at Suka Espresso, three minutes on foot, then your sunlit work nook and 50mbps fiber Wi‑Fi.

  3. 14:00

    The pool, or the surf

    Float on the beach ledge of the 16m pool, or ride three minutes to the waves at Padang Padang.

  4. 18:00

    Sunset on the cliffs

    Live music at Single Fin, four minutes away, or the Kecak fire dance at Uluwatu Temple.

  5. 22:00

    A gentle hush

    Back through the gate, Uluwatu's bustle falls away. Blackout curtains, cool linen, deep sleep.

Alone, Together

Company, without the small talk

Some days you want to be completely alone. Most days you simply want to be around people. With just a handful of studios around one pool, Nema lives in that gentle middle: a nod over morning coffee, a quiet chat on the sunbeds, and a door that closes whenever you want it to.

It is the thing solo travellers tell us they value most. A standalone villa can be beautiful and strangely isolating, with no one to ask for directions, a dinner tip or a driver. Here, our team is on hand day and night, and familiar faces are never far away.

Safe, without trying

Since we opened, the comment we hear most often from women travelling alone is simple: I felt safe here. Discrete 24/7 security and a 24-hour front desk are part of it. Just as important, you never need to scoot. The beach, YUKI, Kynd and dozens of cafes and restaurants are a short walk away, so you are never on a scooter at night.
