Group Stays in Uluwatu

Everyone together, everyone with their own door

Finding a dozen beautiful rooms side by side in Uluwatu is harder than it should be. Groups usually end up scattered across villas and resorts, sharing taxis and group chats. Reserve Nema and your whole party sleeps in one place: twelve private studios around a single 16m pool, a short walk from Thomas Beach.

Every couple or guest gets a private studio with a queen bed, an outdoor rain shower and a balcony. Everyone shares the pool, the garden and the evenings.

The Wedding Party

Getting married on the Uluwatu cliffs? Put your wedding party in one beautiful place. Nema is not a wedding venue; it is where your closest people stay. Breakfast by the pool together, getting-ready mornings, and a short drive to Uluwatu's clifftop venues.

Couples each have their own studio, so the week feels like a celebration rather than a compromise.

02

Hens, Birthdays & Friends

A hen weekend, a 30th, a 40th or a long-overdue trip with old friends. Take over the whole property and make the pool your own private club, then head out to Uluwatu's day clubs and restaurants.

Show your Nema keyring at partner venues such as YUKI, Kynd and Tabu for 10–20% off, or have dinner catered at the villas.

03

Teams & Rewards Trips

Reward your top performers or bring a small team together without big-resort prices. Every studio has a work nook and 50mbps fiber Wi-Fi, there is a 24-hour front desk, and rates are under US$150 per studio per night even in peak season.

Book team dinners through our partner restaurants at a discount, and keep the budget for the experiences people remember.
