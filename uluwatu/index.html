The Neighbourhood

The best of both worlds

Nema sits in the heart of Uluwatu, on the main road, yet the property is tucked just off it down a short lane. Step through the gate and the noise falls away; step back out and Uluwatu's cafes, boutiques and spas are all around you.

Walk to Thomas Beach for a morning swim. Book a reformer class, then brunch at Suka Espresso. Spend the afternoon at a cliffside day club, and watch the sun drop into the ocean from Single Fin before dinner at YUKI.

The Food Capital of Uluwatu

Nema sits at the heart of Uluwatu's food scene. Some of the area's best restaurants are a few minutes' walk from the gate, and your Nema keyring takes 10–20% off at our partners.

  • Kynd CommunityAbout 200m. Famous for its photogenic smoothie bowls and brunch.
  • YUKIAbout 400m. Modern Japanese izakaya, sushi and cocktails; a date-night favourite.
  • Mood by OursA short walk on Jl. Labuansait. Farm-to-table café and organic market.
  • Suka EspressoAbout a 3-minute walk. The local favourite for coffee and a long brunch.
  • Osteria LunaCoastal Italian on the Padang Padang strip, from the team behind YUKI.
  • TabuSleek and atmospheric, made for a long dinner.
02

Sunsets & Day Clubs

Uluwatu faces west, so every evening ends with the sun dropping into the Indian Ocean. Start the day with a swim at Thomas Beach, a short walk away, then choose your cliff.

  • Single FinAbout 4 minutes by ride. Live music above the Uluwatu break.
  • OneEightyThe glass-bottom infinity pool hanging over the cliffs. More champagne than shots.
  • Ulu Cliffhouse & El KabronClifftop pools, long lunches and sunset cocktails.
  • Sundays Beach ClubA full beach day on white sand at the bottom of the cliffs.
  • SavayaUluwatu's headline clifftop club for big DJ nights.
03

Move & Unwind

Local Questions

Essential Details

Thomas Beach is a short walk from Nema down a local pathway, typically under ten minutes on foot.

Suka Espresso is about a three-minute walk for coffee and brunch, and Kynd Community is on the same main road. For dinner, YUKI and Tabu are date-night favourites where Nema guests save 10–20% with their keyring. For sunset, Single Fin is about four minutes away by ride, with cliffside spots like OneEighty, Ulu Cliffhouse and El Kabron along the coast.

Padang Padang Beach is about 3 minutes by scooter or a 20-minute walk. Single Fin at Blue Point is about 4 minutes by scooter, and Uluwatu Temple, with its nightly Kecak fire dance, is 5 to 7 minutes away. Ngurah Rai International Airport is about 17 km away.

Nema is a highly private sanctuary with discrete 24-hour on-site security and a 24-hour front desk. It sits just off the main Uluwatu road, so cafes and restaurants are a short, safe stroll away and you never have to travel far at night.
