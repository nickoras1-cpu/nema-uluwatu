The Neighbourhood

Explore Uluwatu on foot

Nema sits in Pecatu, on Bali's Bukit Peninsula, just a few minutes' stroll to the white sands of Thomas Beach. Uluwatu's renowned culinary scene, elevated cafes, and legendary clifftop venues are at your doorstep, and Bingin's plant-based restaurants and boutique shopping are close by.

It is a neighbourhood made for walking: a morning swim, coffee and a slow vegan breakfast, an afternoon by the pool, and dinner a short stroll from your studio.

01

Thomas Beach

A few minutes' walk from your studio, the white sands of Thomas Beach are made for an early swim, a long read in the shade, or sunset before dinner.

02

Vegan Cafes & Dining

Uluwatu and Bingin are home to some of Bali's best plant-based restaurants and elevated cafes, and many are within walking distance of Nema. Ask our team for their current favourites when you arrive.

03

For Solo Travelers

We prioritize your peace of mind so you can fully relax. Nema is a highly private sanctuary with discrete 24-hour on-site security, and the local dining scene is a short, safe stroll away, meaning you never have to travel far at night.

Our guests often describe Nema as convenient and safe for a first solo trip to Bali.
