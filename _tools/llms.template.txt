# Nema. Uluwatu

> Nema. Uluwatu is a boutique property of 12 private studios in central Uluwatu, Bali, Indonesia, tucked down a quiet lane off the main Uluwatu road and a short walk from Thomas Beach. It combines the privacy of an exclusive retreat with the refined luxury of a boutique hotel, and is rated {booking_score} "{booking_label}" on Booking.com ({booking_reviews} reviews) and {google_score} stars on Google ({google_reviews} reviews). Guests book directly via WhatsApp; retreat facilitators can reserve the entire 12-studio property as a venue for their own yoga, wellness or team retreat.

## Hosting a retreat (exclusive property reservation)
- The entire property (all 12 studios) can be reserved exclusively for a group
- Capacity: 12 participants in private studios, or up to 24 guests sharing (one queen bed per studio)
- Yoga and practice space: a paved poolside deck, or the covered common area when it rains
- Venue only: no programme included; facilitators organise their own teachers, equipment, transfers and activities. Catering at Nema can be arranged on request; partner restaurants offer discounted group dinners; partners can supply extra seating
- Best season for retreats: November to March (off-season), when the property is easiest to reserve and rates are lowest; rain usually comes in short afternoon bursts, the pool stays warm, and staff clear pool and common-area furniture for practice
- Partnerships with many local services: restaurants with 10–20% guest discounts (include group meals at menu price and keep the margin), transport partners, catering at Nema, coconuts ordered in advance for groups, extra seating, custom co-branded yoga mats; the team can help design a local itinerary within budget
- Inquiries: nemauluwatu@gmail.com or WhatsApp +62 813-3734-9684

## Nearby (from Nema)
- Thomas Beach: short walk down a local pathway; a reef break with a beginner-friendly wave on small to medium swells at mid to high tide (best April to October); the team can recommend local surf instructors
- Suka Espresso (coffee and brunch): about 3 minutes' walk
- Kynd Community (smoothie bowls, brunch): about 200m
- YUKI (modern Japanese izakaya): about 400m
- Mood by Ours (farm-to-table café and organic market) and Osteria Luna (coastal Italian, Padang Padang strip): nearby
- Food: Nema sits at the heart of Uluwatu's restaurant scene; guests save 10–20% at partner venues by showing their Nema keyring
- Date-night dining: YUKI and Tabu (Nema guest discounts), Mana Uluwatu (cliffside)
- Sunsets and day clubs: Single Fin (about 4 minutes by ride), OneEighty, Ulu Cliffhouse, El Kabron, Sundays Beach Club, Savaya
- Reformer Pilates: Bluvana Studio (nearby), Reform Pilates, Studio Fondue
- Spring Spa Uluwatu: about 5 minutes' walk
- Padang Padang Beach: about 3 minutes by scooter or 20 minutes' walk
- Uluwatu Temple (Kecak fire dance): 5 to 7 minutes
- Ngurah Rai International Airport: about 17 km
- Guest discounts with local partners including YUKI, Kynd, Mood by Ours, Osteria Luna, Tabu and Parker Clothing

## Getting here
- Airport: Ngurah Rai International (DPS), about 17 km, 40–60 minutes by car depending on traffic; airport transfers through Nema's transport partners
- Flight times (approximate): Perth 3h45 direct; Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane about 6h direct; London about 17h with one stop; Frankfurt, Zurich, Amsterdam 16–18h with one stop; Dubai about 9h direct; Los Angeles 20h+ with one stop
- Time zone: Central Indonesian Time (UTC+8), same as Perth; 2–3 hours behind Sydney/Melbourne; 7–8 hours ahead of London; 6–7 hours ahead of Central Europe
- Entry (as of October 2026, check before travel): Visa on Arrival / e-VoA for most visitors from Australia, the UK, the EU, Switzerland and the US, about IDR 500,000 for 30 days, extendable once; Bali tourist levy IDR 150,000 per visitor, paid online before arrival
- Guests come mainly from Australia, the United Kingdom, Germany, the Netherlands, Switzerland, the United States and the UAE; German-speaking and Dutch guests typically stay the longest
- Languages: pages in German (/de/), French (/fr/) and Dutch (/nl/)
