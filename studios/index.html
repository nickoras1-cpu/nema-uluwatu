The Studios

Twelve private studios, each one a quiet room of your own a few minutes from Thomas Beach.

Inside Every Studio

Designed for rest, made for focus

Where inspiration meets tranquility. Each of our 12 studios is a private, sunlit space with a dedicated work alcove, a queen-sized bed and a signature outdoor bathroom. Every tactile detail is meticulously considered, from high-thread-count linens to bespoke Sensatia Botanicals amenities that elevate your daily rituals.

Architects and designers often single out the small things: curved, softly lit mirrors, hand-finished walls, built-in timber desks and the open sky above every shower. It is a studio made to be lived in, and photographed.
